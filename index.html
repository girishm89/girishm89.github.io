Keeping the strength of OpenEdge, giving users a modern experience.

I'm a Progress 4GL full-stack developer and engineering manager with over 15 years across the full lifecycle of OpenEdge applications, from maintaining mature ADM2 and WebSpeed systems to setting up new environments from scratch with PASOE and the Progress database.

My specialism is connecting OpenEdge to the outside world. I've implemented Single Sign-On and integrated systems with payment gateways, KYC verification services and third-party applications over SOAP, REST and WebHandlers, always with security, reliability and maintainability in mind.

On the frontend I build with React, both full-stack apps on a Progress backend and standalone static web apps. I use VS Code and AI-assisted development with Claude to move faster, raise code quality and make sense of legacy code.
